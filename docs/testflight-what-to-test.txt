Winnow 0.9.2: the same wallet, rewritten in Lean (internal test)

New in 0.9.2

1. Leaving the app no longer loses an answer in flight. In 0.9.1, if the phone locked or you switched apps while Winnow worked (approving "Create an account" on the Ledger, sending a payment), the screen stayed on "Creating…" or "Sending…" even though the work was done. Try: start Create an account with a Ledger, lock the phone while you approve, unlock: the account shows as approved. Send a payment and press Home at once: the receipt shows when you come back.
2. A payment sent just before Winnow goes to the background reaches the network as soon as you return, not a minute later.
3. Advanced mode's Wallet tab no longer jumps while it syncs: scroll down to Transactions and wait through a few syncs.
4. Sign with Ledger says "Approve on the Ledger…" while it waits for the Ledger.
5. After "Sign and continue" the collect step says "Opening the payment…" instead of briefly saying there is no payment.
6. Your own outgoing payments no longer offer "Save sender as person".
7. Backups no longer wait for payments to confirm. The iCloud backup and a backup file now include payments not yet confirmed, with the coins they spend; restoring announces them to the network again. Try: send a payment, back up to a file at once (Settings → Backup), restore it on a second device before the payment confirms: it shows the payment as pending and then confirmed.

This build is Winnow rewritten in Lean 4: the wallet and the app are compiled to native code and drawn with standard iOS controls, with no Swift. It updates the App Store's Winnow Wallet in place and keeps its wallet, receive labels and iCloud backups. Saved people, Signers and multisig accounts from the Swift app are set aside on the phone (not deleted) and start empty: pair your Ledger again under Settings → Signers. Please try it on a phone whose wallet you could restore from its recovery words, and report anything that differs from the Swift app.

Install over the current app

1. With the App Store's Winnow holding a wallet, install this build from TestFlight. The same wallet, balance and Transactions must appear, and it must sync to "Up to date".
2. Settings → Backup: iCloud backup should show "Backed up" with a time after it updates. On a second device signed into the same Apple Account, a fresh install should offer "Restore your iCloud wallet" and restore the same wallet.
3. Settings → Signers → Pair a Ledger works (0.9.0 said "Winnow could not safely read its signers").

Everyday use (Simple mode)

Receive, Send and Activity work as before. Simple mode has no new controls. Tap a payment to see its details; copy its transaction ID.

One Send (Advanced mode)

1. Multisig: "Create an account" chooses who signs (this phone, a Ledger paired under Settings → Signers, keys someone sent); "Join an account" pastes one shared with you. The account page has "Share with co-signers" instead of the descriptor text.
2. Every payment, from the wallet or from an account, starts in Send: pick the account under From. With a recovery path, Send offers "Signers now / Recovery path".
3. Sign and continue, then collect signatures (phone, Ledger, a pasted copy) and finalize. The PSBT to share is BIP174 v0, readable by Bitcoin Core.
4. A payment between the wallet and one of your accounts is one row in Transactions.

Also new: Tor and I2P peers under Settings → Peer networks, background chain checks, and the iPad layout (rotation, sidebar). Mac users with Apple silicon can run the iPad version.

Report bugs at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow-lean/issues with app version, device, Ledger model, network and steps. Never include recovery words, private keys or backup files.
