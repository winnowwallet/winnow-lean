Winnow: the same wallet, rewritten in Lean

New: profiles. A profile is one key and what it holds: its coins, payments, contacts, backup and the multisig accounts its key is in.
1. The home names the open profile: tap it. Switch: instant, no "Syncing" catch-up. Rename it; the name shows on your other devices after a minute.
2. Add a profile (new, or restore another of yours from iCloud). Pay the one that is not open; open it: the payment is already there.
3. The home lists only this profile's accounts and counts only its money; "Watching" holds the others, each saying where it signs.
4. A 2-of-2 of two profiles here: sign in one, tap "Switch to … and sign": the other opens on the payment; add its signature.
5. Leave one device off a while, use the other, then open the first: it should be up to date at once, without scanning the gap.

Since 0.9.21: more of the wallet is proved, and fees are whole-number arithmetic. Send once: the fee quoted must be the fee paid.

Since 0.9.16

Settings → Backup → iCloud backup says how many other backups were read and, for one that was not, why.

Multisig accounts and paired Ledgers follow your devices. With iCloud backup on, they appear on your other devices under the same Apple Account. With Winnow open on both:
1. Join or create an account on one. Within two minutes the other lists it, first as catching up, then with its balance. A device with none of its keys says "Watch-only on this device".
2. Pair a Ledger on one: the other lists it as "Paired on another device" until used there.
3. Start a payment from an account and sign on one: the other shows it awaiting signatures, and a signature added there reaches the first.

Since 0.9.15

Join an account also takes a SegWit v0 account, wsh(sortedmulti(…)), with one signer a single fixed key. On signet, with Bitcoin Core holding the other keys: paste the descriptor (policy "2 of 3", first address Core's), pay that address, then paste Core's PSBT, add this phone's signature, finalize and broadcast.

Since 0.9.11

A card for one person: they pay you at a new address each time. On two phones:
1. Receiving phone: Receive. Under "From one person" type the payer's name and tap the button (Face ID). It shows "An address for …" and "A card for …".
2. Paying phone: Send → Saved recipients → Add. Type a name, paste the card, Save. Send to them twice.
3. The receiving phone must show both as "Received from" that name, at two different addresses.

This build is Winnow rewritten in Lean 4, with no Swift. It updates the App Store's Winnow Wallet in place and keeps its wallet, receive labels and iCloud backups. Saved people, Signers and multisig accounts from the Swift app are set aside on the phone (not deleted) and start empty: pair your Ledger again under Settings → Security → Hardware wallets and keys. Please try it on a phone whose wallet you could restore from its recovery words.

Install over the current app

1. With the App Store's Winnow holding a wallet, install this build from TestFlight. The same wallet, balance and Activity must appear, and it must sync to "Up to date".
2. Settings → Backup: iCloud backup should show "Backed up" with a time. On a second device signed into the same Apple Account, a fresh install should offer "Restore your iCloud wallet" and restore the same wallet.

Report bugs at https://github.com/winnowwallet/winnow-lean/issues with app version, device, Ledger model, network and steps. Never include recovery words, private keys or backup files.
